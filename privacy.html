StealR Privacy Statement

Version 1.0 — 31 October 2026. Nederlandse versie.

  • Delete your account
  • Your rights

1. Who is responsible

GoudFit B.V., Botersteeg 10, 4201 JM Gorinchem, the Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce 94059462), is the controller for the personal data processed in the StealR app. Contact: info@goudfit.nl.

2. What we process, why and for how long

  • Name, email, password (encrypted by our processor): your account and your name on the map and leaderboards. Kept until 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Gender, year of birth: leaderboards per League, gender and age class. Same retention.
  • GPS track during a recording you start yourself: claiming streets, calibration, anti-cheat, Blocks. Same retention; heatmaps are aggregated and anonymous.
  • Heart rate, cadence, power, distance (phone or wearable): calibration, anti-cheat, League placement. Same retention.
  • Motion sensor and step counter: cadence and rep counting at strength checkpoints. Only the count is stored.
  • Imported wearable files (.FIT): StealRTri submissions. Only derived results are stored, never the file.

Location is processed only during a recording. Background use happens only while an activity you started is running, which your phone shows. The app does not use the camera and collects no advertising or tracking data. Nothing can be bought in this version.

3. Where your data lives

At Supabase, in a data centre in the European Union, under a data processing agreement. Data is never sold or used for third-party advertising. If you export an activity as GPX, you decide where that file goes.

4. What other users see

Your name, League, podium places (gold, silver, bronze per street), Blocks and leaderboard rank. Never your exact track.

5. Your rights

Access, correction and export (Settings → Export my data), deletion (below), restriction, objection and a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority. Questions: info@goudfit.nl.

6. Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account, type DELETE to confirm. Claims, activities and profile are removed within 30 days. If the app does not work for you, email info@goudfit.nl from your account's email address.

7. Minors

StealR is intended for people aged 16 and over.

8. Changes

Material changes are announced in the app.

6. Account verwijderen
